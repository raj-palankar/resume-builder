## 🌟 Overview

Creating a professional resume can be difficult, especially for students and people applying for their first internship or job.

**Resume Builder** simplifies the process by providing an easy-to-use interface where users can enter their professional information and create a structured resume.

The project is built as a practical web development application with a focus on **simplicity, usability, clean design, and professional presentation**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👤 Personal Information
Add important personal and contact information required for your resume.

### 🎓 Education
Add your academic qualifications, institutions, courses, and other educational details.

### 💼 Work Experience
Add your professional experience and describe your previous roles and responsibilities.

### 🛠️ Skills
Showcase your technical, professional, and soft skills.

### 🚀 Projects
Highlight projects you have completed and describe the technologies or skills used.

### 🏆 Achievements
Add certifications, achievements, awards, and other important accomplishments.

### 👀 Resume Preview
Review your information and see how your resume looks before using it.

### 📱 Responsive Design
Designed to provide a usable experience across different screen sizes.

### 🎨 Clean Interface
A minimal and professional interface focused on making resume creation straightforward.
